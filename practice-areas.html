Gambling law practice areas

The 20 practice areas that define the regulated gambling legal market — from licensing and regulatory compliance to payments, M&A and responsible gaming.

Licensing

Specialist counsel on licence applications, renewals, variations and suitability across every regulated gaming jurisdiction.

Regulatory Compliance

Ongoing compliance programmes, supervisory engagement and remediation for licensed gaming operators and suppliers.

AML & KYC

Anti-money laundering frameworks, customer due diligence and source-of-funds work for regulated gaming operators.

Corporate & M&A

Transactions, corporate structuring and fundraising for operators, suppliers and investors in the gaming industry.

Payments & Fintech

Payments strategy, PSP structuring and fintech counsel for licensed gaming operators and the providers that serve them.

Data Protection & GDPR

Privacy, data governance and GDPR advice for licensed gaming operators and their service providers.

Intellectual Property

Brand, game IP, licensing and enforcement for operators, suppliers and content producers.

Litigation & Disputes

Tax

Market Entry Strategy

About the firm

Kalff Katz & Franssen — Exclusive boutique specialising 25+ years in gaming and gambling law, media and entertainment sectors.

The firm advises clients across Netherlands, with offices in Amsterdam, Netherlands. Full profile and contact details are available via the firm's website at https://www.kalffkatzfranssen.nl/en/.

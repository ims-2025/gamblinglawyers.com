About the firm

Triay Limited — Gibraltar's longest continuously operating law firm (1905); full-service firm with corporate and commercial expertise; gaming practice not primary focus.

The firm advises clients across Gibraltar, Spain, with offices in Gibraltar, Gibraltar, Marbella, Spain. Full profile and contact details are available via the firm's website at https://www.triay.com.

Practice

The firm's core work covers Corporate & M&A, Commercial Contracts, Litigation & Disputes across Gibraltar, Spain.
